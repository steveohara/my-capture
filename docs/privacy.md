# Privacy Policy for My Capture

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

My Capture is a utility that shows the picture and plays the sound of a USB
video capture device, on macOS, Windows and iPadOS. This page covers all
three versions.

## Summary

My Capture does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It
has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. It
does not record video or audio. It makes no network connections of its own.

## What the app does with your device

- **Video and audio** from the capture device you choose are shown and
  played live, on screen and through your chosen audio output. Nothing is
  written to disk, copied, or sent anywhere.
- On iPadOS, the app asks for Camera and Microphone permission because that
  is how Apple's AVFoundation framework presents a USB video capture device;
  the permission is used only to show and play that live picture and sound,
  the same as the rest of this policy describes.
- Your chosen video device, audio input, aspect mode and rotation are
  remembered on your own device (in `UserDefaults` on iPadOS, or equivalent
  local settings storage on macOS and Windows) purely so the app opens the
  same way next time. This information never leaves your device.

## Network use

The app itself makes no network connections. The "About" screen contains
ordinary links to this project's GitHub page and issue tracker; following
one of those opens your device's own web browser, which is subject to
GitHub's own privacy policy, not this one.

## Children's privacy

My Capture does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at
all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page will change
with it. Given the app's design, any future change is expected to still say
that nothing is collected.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised as a GitHub issue:
