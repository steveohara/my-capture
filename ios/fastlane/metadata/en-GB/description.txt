My Capture turns your iPad into a live monitor for a USB-C video capture card: the kind used with retro game consoles, camcorders, mixing desks, HDMI switches and other capture-card sources. Plug the card into your iPad's USB-C port and the picture and sound appear straight away, full screen, with no recording and no account.

FEATURES

- Picks up the capture card automatically as soon as it is plugged in, alongside the iPad's own built-in cameras.
- Choose the exact mode (resolution and frame rate) the card offers; the highest one up to 1920 pixels wide is chosen first.
- Fit, Stretch, Fill, or force the picture to 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, 21:9 or 1:1.
- A manual Rotate control turns the picture in 90 degree steps, because a capture card's picture has no fixed relationship to how the iPad is held.
- Hear the card's own audio, brought up to a normal listening level, through the speaker, headphones, a Bluetooth device, or AirPlay, chosen with the system's own output picker.
- An adjustable audio delay lines the sound back up with the picture if the two drift apart.
- A Help screen and an About screen with version details, right in the app.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

Nothing is recorded. My Capture has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections of its own. See the in-app Help and the privacy policy for details.

REQUIREMENTS

An iPad running iPadOS 17 or later, with a USB-C port, and a USB Video Class capture device. Most capture cards from a few different brands work this way; My Capture can not guarantee every model, since iPadOS itself decides which USB video and audio devices it recognises.

My Capture is also available for macOS and Windows; see the project's GitHub page from the About screen for those versions.
